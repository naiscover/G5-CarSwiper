## Transparency & Information Sharing
- All relevant information is shared between us.
- Any questions about anything can be asked.

## Active Listening & Feedback 
- Questions regarding the project are considered, even if one member thinks it's a bad idea
- Everyone is free to share their perspective or vision of the project.

## Respect & Trust
- Everyone is treated with respect
- No one's ideas or thought-proccesses are left out nor do we make it feel like they are bad ideas

## Conflict's & Disagreement 
- Disagreements will be discussed throughout the group, and all perspectives will be taken into account

## Channel & Tool Usage
- We will use Microsoft Team's for our main means of communication
- We will use Github as our main source of sharing code

## Meeting Etiquette
- Meeting's will begin and end on scheduled time
- They will be held anytime we feel we need to work on the project
- If a member cannot attend a meeting, reason must be stated
